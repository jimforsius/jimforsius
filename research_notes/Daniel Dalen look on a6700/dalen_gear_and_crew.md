# Daniel Dalen: video production gear, crew and workflow (research notes, compiled 2026-10-12)

**Identity check (important for the report writer):** The brief describes Daniel Dalen as a "Norwegian", "Bangkok/Thailand-based" YouTuber. The primary sources don't support either detail. They do describe the same channel (@danieldalen, channel ID UCwBHuh-snUhlVNUYabe87IQ, which includes the video "Two Really Chaotic Weeks. This is how I stay focused.", 2026-04-19). The person behind it:
- Was born in the Netherlands and raised in Europe and China. Sources: [301 Podcast](https://the301podcast.podbean.com/e/daniel-dalen-every-mentor-has-a-mentor/), [Latitude with Limont, Jul 2023](https://shows.acast.com/the-latitude-podcast/episodes/daniel-dalen), [The Chow, 16 Sep 2025 ("Dutch entrepreneur in Hong Kong")](https://www.thechow.net/p/post-manosphere-daniel-dalen-art-of-mondays).
- Is based in **Hong Kong** and works a lot in mainland China and Dubai.
- Uses the X/Twitter handle @danielvandalen.
- Runs the e-commerce fulfilment company Ecomflow and the eyewear brand LENSE ([danieldalen.com](https://www.danieldalen.com/); [YouTube descriptions](https://www.youtube.com/@danieldalen/videos)).
- Is the subject of his September 2026 video, a live podcast in Amsterdam. Its description says he grew up as "an insecure kid in a small Dutch to[wn]" ([my old home met the new me, 27 Sep 2026](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FfeAr4pgGmM)).

None of these sources ties him to Thailand. Several unrelated people share the name, such as Daniel Dalen of Sun Lighting Phoenix on LinkedIn, and I excluded them.

**Method:**
- I pulled the descriptions of about 37 videos from his channel (2024 to Oct 2026) straight from YouTube page data.
- I pulled auto-caption transcripts of 4 videos with yt-dlp, including both of his dedicated gear videos.
- I resolved every Amazon affiliate link to its product page.
- I read upload frame rates from YouTube's stream manifests for 10 videos. YouTube's bot check blocked the rest.

Tags used below:
- **VERIFIED**: Dalen or his crew said it on camera, or it appears in his own description, gear list or social post.
- **INFERRED**: comes from metadata analysis or third parties.

## Q1. Cameras and lenses (plus mounts, mics and other gear)

### Takeaway
Dalen has published two gear lists, in July 2024 and March 2026, and both name the same two-camera Sony system:
- **Sony FX30 (APS-C) + Sony E 11mm f/1.8.** Used for wide talking-head "education" shots, POV/static clamp shots and match cuts.
- **A full-frame Sony with the FE 24-70mm f/2.8 GM.** Used for "documentation" shots. This was the **A7S III** in 2024 (a crew member's camera) and the **FX3** by 2026.

Supporting cameras include a VHS camcorder, a DJI Osmo Pocket 3, an iPhone and 35mm point-and-shoot film cameras for stills.

## Q3. Diffusion, ND and other optical tricks

### Takeaway
**Yes, both are verified.** In 2024 he says a **"mist filter"** was on the setup, recommended by his friend Hugo, for "a super soft kind of glare". The crew also uses **ND filters** in sun to keep a low f-stop, and bought more NDs in 2026. The brand and strength of the mist filter (Black Pro-Mist, CineBloom, etc.) were never stated.

### Cited Findings
- On the mist filter, Dalen says (VERIFIED) — [YouTube CTkma5c0qCo, 30 Jul 2024](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CTkma5c0qCo):
  - "what I have on here is a… Mist filter which gets recommended by… our dear friend Yugo [Hugo]."
  - "what it does is when you're filming it just kind of like adds a super soft kind of glare to the light and just gives like a really nice cinematic look… it spreads out the lighting."
  - Context: this was said right after the FX30 purchase segment while holding the camera. Which camera carried the filter is ambiguous, most plausibly the FX30.
- On ND, Voytech says (VERIFIED): "there's no filter on it right now but when it's sunny outside we use the ND filter… like sunglasses for your lens… that's how we can also shoot like low f-stop but still… outside when it's sunny" — [YouTube CTkma5c0qCo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CTkma5c0qCo). This refers to the A7S III + 24-70 GM.
- In 2026, Dalen says (VERIFIED): "we just picked up a bunch of new lenses, some ND filters… This ND filter is so nice, bro" — [YouTube bK6283rQ7c0](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bK6283rQ7c0).
- Other "optical" texture comes from mixing in VHS camcorder footage, film-camera stills and iPhone or Osmo Pocket clips (VERIFIED) — [YouTube bK6283rQ7c0](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bK6283rQ7c0).
- Third-party description of the look: "warm, sun-drenched", "eternal golden hour", "filmic quality with warm, colorful tones and a grainy texture", "red flare transitions" — [The Chow, 16 Sep 2025](https://www.thechow.net/p/post-manosphere-daniel-dalen-art-of-mondays).

### Inferences
- The grain and red flare transitions noted by The Chow match the content of Cinegrams bundles, which Dalen endorses (see Q5): "120x Film Grain Scans", "40x Film Flashes & Burns", and light leaks. These effects are likely added in post rather than optically. **INFERRED.**
- The ND use suggests a wide-aperture, shallow-depth-of-field habit in daylight. Combined with the stated aim of a "cinematic" look, this is consistent with keeping the shutter near 180°, although no source states it. **INFERRED.**

### Gaps
- Brand, grade and size of the mist filter: not stated anywhere I found.
- Whether a mist filter is still used in 2026: not stated.
- Variable or fixed ND, and brand: not stated.
- I found no mention of Glimmerglass, CineBloom, anamorphic adapters or vintage lenses.
